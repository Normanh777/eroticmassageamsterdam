Questions about our packages

Is a happy ending included in the packages?

Every package ends with an erotic massage of your choice, and a happy ending is part of our erotic massages.

What is included in the price?

The price is for the time of your masseuse in Amsterdam, Schiphol, Haarlem or Hoofddorp. Restaurant bills, drinks, champagne and tickets for a cruise or museum are paid by you. For other cities see the prices page.

Can I change a package?

Yes. Every package is a starting point. Add an hour, choose another massage or combine two packages, and we confirm the rate in the chat before you book.

How do I book a package?

Open the chat, tell us which package you would like, your hotel or address, the date and time, and the masseuse you prefer. We confirm everything before it is final.
